* Revenue & Pricing

1- What is the total drafted revenue (sum of AgreedPrice) across all bookings?
2- What is the average booking value, and how does it vary by layout (1BR, 2BR, 3BR)?
3- Are there significant differences between OriginalPrice vs AgreedPrice, indicating negotiation trends?
4- What is the revenue distribution by project (ProjectId)?

============================================

* Stage-Level Analysis
1- How many bookings stay in Draft without progressing to Deals?
2- What is the average time a booking stays in Draft before moving forward?
    (You can compare CreationDate to next stage later when available.)

============================================

* Workload Monitoring

1- How many draft bookings were created per day/week/month?
2- Are there booking spikes around specific times?

============================================

* Agent Productivity

1- Which agents create the highest number of bookings?
2- What is the total revenue attributed to each agent?
3- Is there any agent with higher differences between Original and Agreed prices? 

============================================

* Manager & Leadership Oversight

1- Which managers oversee the highest-value bookings?
2- Is any manager generating more luxury or high-price layouts?

============================================

* Brokerage Insights

1- Which brokerage generates the highest number of bookings?
2- Which broker contributes the highest revenue?
3- Are some brokers consistently booking more 2BR/3BR units compared to others?
4- Are certain brokers associated with higher discounts?

============================================
 
* Customer Segmentation

1- Are customers more likely to book 1BR, 2BR, or 3BR units?
2- How do customer choices differ by project?

============================================

* Demographic or Pattern Behavior (based on additional data)

1- What times or days do customers most frequently make bookings?

============================================

* Layout Demand

1- Which layout type (1BR, 2BR, 3BR) is most in demand?
2- What is the average price per layout?

============================================

* Project-Level Market Behavior

1- Which projects have the highest-value bookings?
2- Are certain projects being booked at a higher velocity?

============================================

* Conversion to Deal

1- What is the conversion rate from Booking → Deal per agent?
2- Which stage causes most drop-off?

============================================

* Useful for internal audits or system behavior.

1- Are there any duplicate UnitIds being booked by different customers? (Example: UnitId 3940 appears twice in your data.)
2- Are agents submitting bookings with missing fields (like CreatedBy = "")?
3- Are there bookings with unusually high or low prices indicating anomalies?
